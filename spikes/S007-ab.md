# S007 A/B: parakeet against whisper in the worker, on the lab (spec 007, AC16)

## Verdict
**parakeet passes every AC16 bound**, on the rerun after the forced-cut dating fix (`fix(stt): date a forced cut at the cut`). The first run missed one-talker p95 (4.01 s and 4.90 s against ≤ 4 s). There, a 15 s forced cut was dated 800 ms before the cut, as if a pause had ended it, in the replay and in `grill_stt_line_latency_seconds` alike. The user dropped "at or below whisper" for the Piper English lines (spec 007, Decisions), leaving WER ≤ 0.08.

| AC16 check | parakeet | bound | pass |
|---|---|---|---|
| meetings WER | 0.230 | ≤ 0.25, ≤ whisper 0.317 | yes |
| talk WER | 0.122 | ≤ 0.15, ≤ whisper 0.216 | yes |
| Piper English WER | 0.047 | ≤ 0.08 | yes |
| English leak, meetings / talk | 0.00 / 0.00 | ≤ 0.01 | yes |
| p95, one talker | 3.30 s | ≤ 4 s | yes |
| p95, two talkers | 3.84 s | ≤ 5 s | yes |
| p95, four talkers | 3.89 s, no drop | target 8 s, bound 10 s | yes |
| peak pod working set | 2.56 GiB (four talkers) | ≤ 2.7 GiB | yes |

whisper, the rollback path, comes from the first run, before the fix, so its latencies are overstated by up to 0.8 s on forced cuts: meetings 0.317, talk 0.216, p95 7.0 s with one talker, 8.1 s with two and 18.5 s with four. Its filter dropped 34 meeting lines.

## Setup
- The stt image itself, `python -m grill_stt.replay`: whole speaker tracks played in 10 ms frames on the wall clock, through the worker's Segmenter (800 ms, 15 s), its two engine processes of 2 threads, its queue, per-speaker ordering and filter. Latency runs from the end of speech, as `grill_stt_line_latency_seconds` measures it, to the line handed to the room API.
- One Job per engine and load, at the 4-CPU limit with a 900m request and 3Gi, one Job per node (pod anti-affinity), on the lab's 4-vCPU AVX2 workers. CFS throttling stayed at zero in every run.
- Corpora as in S10d, uncut: SUMM-RE meetings (14 speaker tracks, 62 min of track, gold reference, the S10d bleed filter applied to the posted lines), two 3-min clips of the French tech talk (silver reference), and the 38 Piper lines. Talkers 2 and 4 play the meeting tracks in groups of that size, longest first. One-talker plays every set in sequence.
- Scoring is S10b's: hesitations removed, WER per set, English leak as the share of words in English-dominated sentences of French items.
- The first one-talker run landed on w01, which the other tenants loaded more: there, each audio second took 30 % more compute than on w02. It was rerun on w02 with the two-talker node pinned. Memory is the working set (`memory.current - inactive_file`), the figure kubelet uses. `memory.peak` also counts the corpus and model page cache and climbs to the 3Gi limit.

- The meetings WER matches S10d exactly (0.232 there, on cuts the bench prepared). The live Segmenter, queue and filter change nothing in accuracy.

## Reproduce
Files in `spikes/s007-ab/`:
- `prep.py` builds the uncut corpus. It needs the talk's audio (yt-dlp) and the S10d caption reference.
- `job.yaml` and `run.sh` run one Job per engine and load. `NODE` pins a node.
- `score.py` scores the outputs and prints the AC16 table.

```bash
uv run --no-project -p 3.12 --with pyarrow --with soundfile --with soxr --with huggingface_hub --with numpy \
  --with piper-tts==1.8.0 python spikes/s007-ab/prep.py <corpus> <talk.m4a> <devoxx_items.json>
# copy <corpus> into the PVC as /data/ab, then:
export KUBECONFIG=<lab kubeconfig> NS=<namespace> OUT=<log dir> IMAGE=<stt image by digest>
spikes/s007-ab/run.sh p1 parakeet 900m summre:1 devoxx:1 piper:1
spikes/s007-ab/run.sh p24 parakeet 900m summre:2 summre:4    # and w1, w24 with whisper
python3 spikes/s007-ab/score.py <corpus> <dir of the Jobs' /data/out>
```
