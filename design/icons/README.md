# grill-room icon proposals

Palette: ember `#F4512C`, butter `#FFC23D`, teal `#159F8C`, ink `#1E2238`, white.
Each icon uses at most three colors plus ink or white.

![grill-bubble](01-grill-bubble.svg) Speech bubble seared with three grill marks.
![talking-kettle](02-talking-kettle.svg) Kettle grill with its lid open, chatting.
![round-table](03-round-table.svg) Top view of a table, five seats, one shared page.
![flame-page](04-flame-page.svg) A document catching the heat of the discussion.
![voice-to-page](05-voice-to-page.svg) Voice waveform turning into lines of text.
![grill-mic](06-grill-mic.svg) Microphone whose mesh is a grill grate.
![margin-note](07-margin-note.svg) Highlighted line with a comment bubble in the margin.
![heat-dial](08-heat-dial.svg) Debate thermometer, needle pushed into the hot zone.
![gr-monogram](09-gr-monogram.svg) G and R sharing one stroke, drawn as paths.
![idea-skewer](10-idea-skewer.svg) Ideas threaded on a skewer, ready for the fire.
![sizzle-overlap](11-sizzle-overlap.svg) Two voices overlap; the overlap is where it sizzles.
![spatula-flip](12-spatula-flip.svg) Slotted spatula flipping a page to its next revision.
![hot-seat](13-hot-seat.svg) A chair with a flame on it: the hot seat.
![seared-revisions](14-seared-revisions.svg) Stack of revisions, the latest one seared.
![open-door](15-open-door.svg) Open door into a warm room where people talk.
![grate-meter](16-grate-meter.svg) Round grill grate whose bars double as audio levels.
![tongs-grab](17-tongs-grab.svg) Tongs picking up a speech bubble.
![smoke-signals](18-smoke-signals.svg) Campfire whose smoke rises as speech bubbles.
![quote-flames](19-quote-flames.svg) Opening quote marks shaped like two flames.
![grill-house](20-grill-house.svg) A room, literally, with grill marks on it.

2. **19 quote-flames.** Two shapes, no detail to lose, and it carries a double meaning.
